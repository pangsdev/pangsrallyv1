1. Claim your seat

Free for Beta pass holders. Register once, and you are in every qualifying round. It uses none of your daily races.

2. Race four rounds

Every round shuffles you into a new room of 10. Race points go on a live Cup table. If you close the page, your race still runs.

3. Make the Grand Final

The top 10 go through and everyone restarts on zero. One race decides the Cup: the final result sets the prizes.

Every racer has equal stats, so luck decides each race. Ties for the final: most wins, then most second places, then a seeded draw. Team wallets cannot win prizes. Full rules
