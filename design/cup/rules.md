# Pangs Cup #1: official rules (draft)

Draft for the owner's review and legal check. Date, time and prizes are samples until confirmed.

1. **Entry.** Free. No payment is needed or accepted. One seat per person and per wallet.
2. **Who can enter.** Wallets that hold the Pangs Rally Beta pass. The team can limit a Cup to wallets that joined the game before it was announced. Team wallets cannot enter.
3. **Seats.** Up to 50 seats, first come, first served (we may add seats while registration is open). Registration closes 30 minutes before Round 1. The Cup needs at least 10 registered racers: with fewer, it is postponed to a new date, and everyone registered keeps their seat.
4. **Qualifying.** Four rounds. Each round puts racers into new random rooms of 10. Places score 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1 points. Cup points do not count towards the Beta Season, and Cup races use none of a player's daily races.
5. **Grand Final.** The top 10 on Cup points go through. Ties at the cut: most wins, then most second places, then a seeded draw. Everyone starts the final on zero, and the final result sets the prizes.
6. **Prizes.** A 30 USDG prize pool. 1st: 15 USDG and a Genesis whitelist place. 2nd: 10 USDG and a Genesis whitelist place. 3rd: 5 USDG and a Genesis whitelist place. Every finalist (1st to 10th) gets a soulbound Pangs Cup badge NFT on Robinhood Chain. Prizes have no link to `$PANG`.
7. **Fairness.** Every racer has equal stats, so luck decides each race. The server decides every result from one secret fixed before Round 1. Its SHA-256 fingerprint is posted before the Cup, and the secret is published after the Grand Final, so anyone can recheck every room draw and every race.
8. **Leaving the page.** A race runs even if a player closes the page, and the result counts.
9. **Cheating.** Using several wallets to hold more than one seat removes that person from the Cup and from any prize.
10. **Claiming.** After the Grand Final the results are written on chain. Each finalist claims the badge, and any USDG, from the wallet they raced with, on the Cup page (gas only). USDG not claimed within 30 days returns to the prize wallet; the badge can be claimed at any time.
11. **Problems.** Rounds run on the server at their set time, whoever is watching. If a technical fault stops the Cup, it is run again on a new date and everyone is told in advance.
